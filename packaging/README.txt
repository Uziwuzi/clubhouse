CLUBHOUSE 1.8.2
A franchise companion for MLB The Show 26 (Switch version, played through an emulator on a Windows PC).

HOW TO START
1. Keep this whole folder together. Clubhouse.exe needs the "_internal" folder next to it.
2. Double-click Clubhouse.exe.
3. The first time, pick the folder where your emulator keeps MLB The Show 26 saves
   (the folder that holds your FRAN... save folders, or the profile folder above them).
4. Leave Clubhouse open while you play. When you save in the game, it asks whether to load the new save.

WHAT IT DOES
Standings, stats, award races, team grades, series plans, the draft board and your draft class,
free agency, the trade evaluator, September call-ups, the whole minor-league system, player cards
with career history, and more, all read from your own franchise save.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Clubhouse only reads your save. It never changes it.
- Nothing goes online. Its settings and franchise history are kept in %APPDATA%\Clubhouse.
- Franchise history starts the first time Clubhouse reads each season, so open it at least once a season.
- Projections (trade value, playoff odds, awards, call-up ETAs, press conferences, media) are
  Clubhouse's own estimates, not the game's.
- Needs Windows 10 or 11 with Microsoft Edge WebView2 (already installed on almost every PC).
  If the window stays blank, install "WebView2 Runtime" from Microsoft.
- Only works with the Switch version's saves. PlayStation, Xbox and PC saves are stored differently.
